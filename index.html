📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be really hard. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. It's a way to make investing more consistent and less stressful.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water: you turn on the faucet at a steady rate, and the bucket gets filled up over time, regardless of whether it's raining or sunny outside. You're not trying to guess when the best time to fill the bucket is, you're just filling it up a little bit at a time.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk by ignoring market ups and downs.
