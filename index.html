📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular times, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when the price is low and fewer shares when the price is high. Over time, this can help reduce the overall cost of your investments.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying oranges at the grocery store. You decide to buy 10 oranges every week, no matter if they're on sale or not. Some weeks you might get them for $1 each, and other weeks they might be $2 each. But over time, you'll average out the cost and get a good deal.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce the impact of market ups and downs, making it a smart long-term investment strategy.
