📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult even for experts.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like regularly adding a fixed amount of money to a piggy bank, regardless of how much candy costs at the store. Sometimes you'll get more candy for your money, and sometimes you'll get less, but over time, you'll have a lot of candy and a full piggy bank.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and reduce risk by ignoring market ups and downs and focusing on long-term goals.
