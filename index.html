📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you buy a fixed amount of food every week, regardless of whether the prices are high or low, to ensure you have a steady supply. Similarly, dollar cost averaging helps you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, smoothing out the ups and downs of the market.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk, by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
