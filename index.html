📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the bumps in the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term changes.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the prices are high, sometimes they're low. You still buy the same amount of food each week, so when prices are low, you get more, and when prices are high, you get less. It's the same with dollar cost averaging - you're buying a fixed amount of 'food' (stocks) at a fixed interval, regardless of the 'price' (market conditions).
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
