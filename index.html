📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult even for experts.

Think of it like: Imagine you're filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow. You can't control the speed of the water, but if you keep the faucet on at the same rate, you'll eventually fill up the bucket, no matter what. In the same way, dollar cost averaging helps you fill up your investment bucket steadily, without worrying about the ups and downs of the market.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and build wealth over time, regardless of market conditions.
