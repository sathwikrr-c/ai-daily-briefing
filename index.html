📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market, so you're not trying to guess when to invest. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market perfectly. By doing this, you can reduce the impact of market volatility on your investments.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up your gas tank - you put in $20 every week, whether gas prices are high or low, so you're averaging out the cost over time. You're not trying to guess when gas prices will be lowest, you just consistently put in your $20.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce the risk of trying to time the market, by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals.
