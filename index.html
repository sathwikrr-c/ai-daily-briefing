📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult to do correctly.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - you just keep putting in the same amount of water at the same time every day, no matter how fast or slow the faucet is flowing, and eventually your bucket will get full
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make your investments more stable over time.
