📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once, when the market might be high. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make investing more manageable. It's a long-term strategy that can help you make the most of your investments.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a tap that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - you still get the same amount of water in the end, but you're not affected by the fluctuations in the flow
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing more manageable, regardless of market conditions.
