📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once when the market is high. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when the market is low and fewer shares when the market is high. Over time, this can help you get a better average price for your investments.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't try to time when to buy milk based on its price, you just buy it every week because you need it, and sometimes you get a better deal, sometimes you don't, but overall you get what you need at a fair price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and reduce risk by ignoring market ups and downs.
