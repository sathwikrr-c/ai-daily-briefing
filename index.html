📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market over time.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the prices are high, sometimes they're low. You still buy the same amount of food each week, regardless of the price, so over time, you get a fair average price. It's the same with dollar cost averaging - you're investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, so you get a fair average price for your investments.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps reduce risk by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
