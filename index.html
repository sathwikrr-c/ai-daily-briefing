📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing money in the stock market where you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once, when the market might be high or low. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce stress. It's a long-term strategy that helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you keep buying the same amount because you need them. It's the same with dollar cost averaging, you invest the same amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
