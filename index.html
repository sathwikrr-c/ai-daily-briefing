📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, ongoing savings plan, but for investing in stocks. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't try to time when to buy milk based on its price, you just buy it every week because you need it. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's price.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce the risk of investing in the stock market.
