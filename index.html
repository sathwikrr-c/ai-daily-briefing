📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing money in the stock market where you put in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't try to buy all your food for the year at once, you buy a little every week, regardless of whether prices are high or low. This way, you average out the cost over time.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
