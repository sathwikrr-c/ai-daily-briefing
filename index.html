📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent plan that helps you build wealth over time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable and stressful.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you keep buying the same amount because you need them, and over time, you average out the costs.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and build wealth over time, regardless of market conditions.
