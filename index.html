📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for investing in the stock market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for the price of milk to go down before you buy it, you just buy it every week because you need it. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
