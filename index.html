📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once, when the market might be high. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when the market is low and fewer shares when the market is high, which can help you get a better average price over time. This strategy can help you invest consistently and avoid trying to time the market.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like buying groceries every week, no matter if they're on sale or not. You're consistently putting in the same amount of money, and over time, you'll get a good average price for your groceries, even if some weeks they're more expensive than others.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk by averaging out the highs and lows of the market over time.
