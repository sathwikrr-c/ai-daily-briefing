📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing in the stock market where you put in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be really hard. By doing this, you can reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. It's a long-term strategy that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for your favorite cereal to go on sale before you buy it, you just buy it every week at the regular price. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you reduce risk by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, rather than trying to time the market with a large sum of money.
