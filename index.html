📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once when the market might be high. By investing a little at a time, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce the impact of market ups and downs. It's a long-term strategy that helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like buying groceries every week. You don't stop buying food just because the price of your favorite snack went up one week - you keep buying what you need, and over time, you average out the cost. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you keep investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, and over time, you average out the cost of your investments.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk by averaging out the cost of your investments over time.
