📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and just focus on investing regularly.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up your gas tank - you put in a fixed amount of gas at regular intervals, regardless of whether gas prices are high or low, to ensure you always have enough to get where you need to go.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, reducing your risk and increasing your chances of long-term success.
