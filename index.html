📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Bull vs Bear Market

A bull market is when the stock market is doing well and prices are going up. This means people are feeling good about the economy and are buying stocks, which drives the prices higher. On the other hand, a bear market is when the stock market is doing poorly and prices are going down. This happens when people are worried about the economy and are selling their stocks, which drives the prices lower.

Think of it like: Imagine you're at a party and everyone is having a great time, dancing and laughing - that's like a bull market. But if the music stops and people start leaving, that's like a bear market.
💬 Remember: A bull market is a time of growth and optimism, while a bear market is a time of caution and decline.
