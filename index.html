📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a savings plan, but instead of putting money in a bank, you're investing it in the stock market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you just keep buying what you need. It's the same with dollar cost averaging, you keep investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps reduce risk by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
