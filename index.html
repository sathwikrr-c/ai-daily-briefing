📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for stocks. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and just focus on investing regularly.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at the same time every week, no matter what the price of gas is. You're not trying to guess when gas will be cheapest, you're just making sure you have enough gas to get where you need to go.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make your investments more stable over time.
