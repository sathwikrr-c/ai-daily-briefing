📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once when the market might be high. Over time, you'll buy more shares when the price is low and fewer shares when the price is high, which can help you save money. It's a simple and steady approach to investing that can help you avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market swings.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the price of your favorite food is high, but sometimes it's low. You still buy the same amount of groceries every week, so when the price is low, you get more food for your money, and when the price is high, you get less. Dollar cost averaging works in a similar way, helping you smooth out the ups and downs of the market.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can reduce risk and increase your chances of long-term success.
