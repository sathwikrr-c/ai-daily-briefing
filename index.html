📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter the market's performance. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investment. For example, you might invest $100 every month, whether the market is high or low. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when prices are low and fewer shares when prices are high, which can help you save money in the long run.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries: you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, and you don't buy extra just because they're low. You keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and that's similar to how dollar cost averaging works with investments.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time.
