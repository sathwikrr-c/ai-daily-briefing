📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. By doing this, you're not trying to time the market or guess when it will go up or down. Instead, you're just steadily investing a fixed amount of money over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water: you turn on the faucet at the same rate every day, regardless of whether it's raining or sunny outside. Over time, your bucket will fill up with water, just like your investments will grow over time with dollar cost averaging.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can be a great way to build wealth over the long term.
