📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to guess when the market will go up or down. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. This way, you'll buy more shares when the price is low and fewer shares when the price is high, which can help you save money in the long run.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like buying groceries every week, regardless of whether they're on sale or not. You're not trying to guess when the prices will be low, you're just buying what you need at a steady pace. This way, you'll get some weeks where you save money and some weeks where you pay a bit more, but overall you'll be consistent and avoid running out of food.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can reduce your risk and increase your chances of long-term success.
