📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. It's a long-term strategy that can help you build wealth over time.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow. You turn on the faucet at the same time every week, so sometimes you get a lot of water and sometimes you get a little, but over time the bucket fills up steadily.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
