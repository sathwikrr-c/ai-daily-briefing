📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be tricky. By investing a little bit of money at a time, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. This way, you can invest with confidence, knowing you're not putting all your eggs in one basket.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at the same time every week, regardless of the price of gas. Sometimes you'll get a good deal, and sometimes you won't, but over time, you'll average out the cost and be able to drive wherever you need to go.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing more manageable.
