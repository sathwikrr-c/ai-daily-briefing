📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once, when the market might be high. By investing a little bit of money regularly, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce the impact of market ups and downs. This way, you can invest with less stress and more consistency.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your car's gas tank a little bit every week, rather than trying to fill it up all at once when gas prices might be high. You'll get a better overall deal and avoid the stress of trying to time the perfect fill-up.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing more manageable, regardless of market conditions.
