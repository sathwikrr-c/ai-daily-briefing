📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once, when the market might be high. By investing a little bit at a time, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce the impact of market ups and downs. This way, you can invest steadily and consistently, without worrying about the best time to invest.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at the same time every week, regardless of the price of gas. Sometimes you'll get a good deal, and sometimes you won't, but over time, you'll average out the cost and ensure you always have gas in your tank.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing more manageable.
