📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term fluctuations. It's a long-term strategy that can help you build wealth over time.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like regularly adding a fixed amount of money to a piggy bank, regardless of whether it's a good or bad week. You're not trying to guess when the best time to add money is, you're just consistently adding to it, and over time, it grows.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
