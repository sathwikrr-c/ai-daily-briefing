📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. By investing a little bit at a time, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable. This approach can help you invest steadily and sensibly over time.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at the same time every week, regardless of whether gas prices are high or low. You're not trying to guess when gas will be cheapest; you just need to keep your tank full, and over time, you'll average out the cost.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you smooth out market fluctuations and build wealth over the long term.
