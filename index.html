📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once, when the market might be high. By investing a little bit at a time, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions. This approach can help you invest steadily and consistently over time.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow. You turn on the faucet at the same time every week, and the bucket gets filled up gradually, no matter how fast or slow the water is flowing.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing less intimidating.
