📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large amount of money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you average out the costs.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
