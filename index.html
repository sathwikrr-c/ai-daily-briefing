📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting a fixed amount of money into the market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for investments. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for the price of milk to go down before you buy it, you just buy a gallon every week because you need it. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk, by spreading your investments over time and avoiding the temptation to try to time the market.
