📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable and stressful.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow. You keep putting in the same amount of water at the same time every day, and over time, the bucket gets full, regardless of how fast or slow the faucet is flowing.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid trying to time the market, making it a smart and low-stress way to build wealth over the long term.
