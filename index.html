📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for investing in the stock market. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you wouldn't try to time the market to buy milk when it's cheapest, you just buy it every week because you need it. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you're investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk, by removing the need to try to time the market perfectly.
