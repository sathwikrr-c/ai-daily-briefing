📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very tricky.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you just keep buying what you need at the current price, and over time, you average out the cost. It's the same with dollar cost averaging in the stock market.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and consistently, reducing the impact of market ups and downs on your investments.
