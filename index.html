📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult to do.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and some weeks the prices are high, but some weeks they're low. You still need to buy the same amount of groceries, so you just keep buying, no matter what the price is. Over time, you'll end up with a good average price, and that's kind of like what happens with dollar cost averaging in the stock market.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can reduce your risk and increase your chances of long-term success.
