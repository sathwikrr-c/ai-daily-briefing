📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once when the market might be high. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions. It's a steady and consistent approach to investing.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you buy a fixed amount of food every week, regardless of whether the prices are high or low, to ensure you have a steady supply. Similarly, dollar cost averaging helps you build a steady investment portfolio over time.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk by avoiding attempts to time the market.
