📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for investing in the stock market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and just focus on investing regularly.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you average out the costs.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk by avoiding the temptation to try to time the market.
