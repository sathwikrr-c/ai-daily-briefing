📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries every week - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, and you don't buy extra just because they're low. You just keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you get a good average price.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and lead to long-term financial success.
