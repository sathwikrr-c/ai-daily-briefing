📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market perfectly. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market swings.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the price of your favorite snack went up one week. You keep buying what you need, at the price it is, and over time, you get a fair average price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid timing risks, by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
