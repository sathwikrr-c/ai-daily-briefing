📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large amount of money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan, rather than trying to time the market perfectly. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market over time.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, but instead of trying to guess when prices will be lowest, you just buy the same amount every week. Sometimes you'll get a good deal, and sometimes you won't, but over time, you'll average out the costs.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, reducing your risk and increasing your chances of long-term success.
