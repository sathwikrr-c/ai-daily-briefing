📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market and avoid trying to time when to invest. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you build wealth over time. By doing this, you can reduce the impact of market volatility on your investments.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - you keep adding water at a steady rate, and over time, the bucket gets full, regardless of the faucet's speed
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you ride out market fluctuations and build wealth over the long term.
