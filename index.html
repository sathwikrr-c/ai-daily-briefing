📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for stocks. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce stress.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you keep buying the same amount because you need them, and over time, you average out the cost, that's what dollar cost averaging does for your investments.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing less stressful.
