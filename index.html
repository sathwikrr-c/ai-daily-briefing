📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market ups and downs. By doing this, you can smooth out the bumps in the market and make investing less stressful.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at the same time every week, no matter if gas prices are high or low. You're putting in the same amount of money, but the amount of gas you get varies - just like how the number of shares you buy varies with the market price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
