📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the market at regular intervals, no matter what the price is. This helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market, so you're not trying to time everything perfectly. It's like a steady, long-term plan, rather than trying to make a quick profit. By doing this, you can reduce the impact of market volatility on your investments.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the price of milk goes up one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you average out the costs.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, reducing the risk of making emotional investment decisions.
