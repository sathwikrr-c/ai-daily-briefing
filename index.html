📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for stocks. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and just focus on investing regularly.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you get a good average price.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce the risk of investing in the stock market.
