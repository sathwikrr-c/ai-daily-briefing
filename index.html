📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable. By doing this, you can reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's a steady and consistent approach to investing.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like regularly adding a fixed amount of money to a piggy bank, regardless of the weather outside - sometimes you'll get more coins for your money when it's 'raining' (market is low), and sometimes you'll get fewer when it's 'sunny' (market is high), but overall, your piggy bank will grow steadily over time.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
