📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular times, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to guess when the market will go up or down. By doing this, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan to build your wealth.

Think of it like: Think of it like regularly adding water to a bucket, whether it's raining or sunny outside - you're steadily filling the bucket, regardless of the weather, and eventually, it will be full.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments.
