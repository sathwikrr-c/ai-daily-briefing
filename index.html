📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. It's a strategy that helps you invest steadily and consistently over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - you turn on the faucet at regular intervals, so you get a steady amount of water in your bucket, no matter how fast or slow it's flowing
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make your investments more consistent over time.
