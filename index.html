📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market perfectly. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for the price of milk to go down before you buy it, you just buy it every week because you need it, and over time, you average out the cost. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you invest a fixed amount regularly, regardless of the market's performance, to average out the cost of your investments.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid trying to time the market, reducing the risk of making emotional and potentially costly investment decisions.
