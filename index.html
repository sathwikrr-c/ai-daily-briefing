📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very tricky.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you keep buying the same amount every week, and over time, you get a good average price. It's similar with dollar cost averaging, you invest a fixed amount regularly, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and avoid trying to time the market.
