📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once when the market is high. By investing a little bit at a time, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult. It's a simple and steady approach to growing your wealth over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - you just keep the faucet on and let the bucket fill up over time, regardless of the water flow
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you smooth out market ups and downs and build wealth over time.
