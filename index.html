📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the bumps in the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult to do.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the prices are high, but sometimes they're low. You still need to buy the same amount of food every week, so you just keep buying, regardless of the price. Over time, you'll average out the costs and get a good overall price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to more consistent and stable long-term returns.
