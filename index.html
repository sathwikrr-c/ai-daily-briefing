📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, long-term plan that doesn't get distracted by short-term market fluctuations. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce stress.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries every week, regardless of whether your favorite food is on sale or not - you're consistently putting money into your 'grocery account' to ensure you have what you need over time.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to guess the market's ups and downs, which can lead to more stable, long-term growth.
