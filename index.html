📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations. It's a strategy that helps you invest steadily and consistently over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you average out the costs.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk by removing the need to perfectly time the market.
