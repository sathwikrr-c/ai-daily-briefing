📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. By investing a little bit at a time, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult. This approach helps you invest steadily and consistently, without worrying about the best time to buy or sell.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - by turning on the faucet at regular intervals, you'll end up with a full bucket over time, regardless of the water flow rate.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and consistently, reducing the impact of market fluctuations on your investments.
