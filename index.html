📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations. It's a long-term strategy that can help you build wealth over time.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you just keep buying the same amount every week, and over time, you get a good average price. It's the same with dollar cost averaging, you're buying a fixed amount of stocks at regular intervals, regardless of the price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
