📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market perfectly. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for the price of milk to be at its lowest to buy it, you just buy it regularly because you need it. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
