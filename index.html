📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be really hard. It's like setting up a monthly transfer from your checking account to your savings account, but instead, you're investing in the stock market. By doing this, you can reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like buying groceries every week. You don't stop buying groceries just because the price of milk went up one week, and you don't buy extra groceries just because they're on sale. You just keep buying what you need, at the current price, and over time, you get a pretty good average price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
