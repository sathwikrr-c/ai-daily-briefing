📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. For example, you could invest $100 every month, whether the market is high or low. This way, you'll buy more shares when the price is low and fewer shares when the price is high, which can help you save money in the long run.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying the same amount every week, and sometimes you get a better deal, sometimes you don't, but on average, you get a fair price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest regularly and reduce timing risks, so you can build wealth over time without worrying about market ups and downs.
