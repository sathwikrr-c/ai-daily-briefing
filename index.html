📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter the market's performance. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. By doing this, you're spreading out your investments over time, which can help you avoid trying to time the market. It's a simple and steady approach to investing that can help you build wealth over the long term.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like regularly adding a fixed amount of money to a piggy bank, regardless of whether it's a good day or a bad day outside. You're not trying to catch the best day to add money, you're just consistently adding to it, and over time, it grows.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you smooth out market fluctuations and build wealth over time.
