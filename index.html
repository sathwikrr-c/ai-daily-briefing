📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the bumps in the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult to do.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the price of bread went up one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace. It's the same with dollar cost averaging, you keep investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to more consistent and less stressful investing.
