📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large amount of money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but instead of saving cash, you're buying stocks. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and just focus on investing regularly.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at the same time every week, regardless of the price of gas. Sometimes you'll get a good deal, and sometimes you won't, but over time, you'll have a full tank and you'll have averaged out the cost.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to lower risk and more stable returns over the long term.
