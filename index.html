📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you build wealth over time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult to do.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at the same time every week, no matter what the price of gas is. Sometimes you'll get a good deal, and sometimes you won't, but over time you'll average out the cost and be able to drive where you need to go.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to more consistent and less stressful investing.
