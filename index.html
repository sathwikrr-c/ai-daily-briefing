📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be tricky. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's a long-term strategy that can help you build wealth over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water: you add a little water at a time, and eventually, the bucket gets full. If you try to add all the water at once, you might spill some, but by adding it slowly, you can fill the bucket without making a mess.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest regularly and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to more stable and consistent returns over the long term.
