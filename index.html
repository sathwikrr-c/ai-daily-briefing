📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like setting up a monthly transfer from your bank account to your investment account. By doing this, you'll be investing a little bit of money regularly, which can help you smooth out the ups and downs of the market.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't try to time when to buy milk based on whether it's on sale or not, you just buy it every week because you need it. Investing with dollar cost averaging is similar, you're consistently putting money into the market, regardless of its current state.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps reduce risk by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
