📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be risky. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's a long-term strategy that can help you build wealth over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water: you add a little water at a time, and eventually, the bucket gets full, regardless of whether it's raining or sunny outside. In the same way, dollar cost averaging helps you fill up your investment bucket with a steady stream of money, regardless of what the market is doing.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you reduce risk and build wealth over time.
