📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable and stressful.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the prices are high, but sometimes they're low. You still need to buy the groceries, so you just keep buying the same amount every week, regardless of the price. Over time, you'll average out the cost and get a good deal.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to lower risk and more stable returns over the long term.
