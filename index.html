📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market and make investing less scary.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow. You turn on the faucet at the same time every day and let it fill the bucket, regardless of the water flow. Over time, your bucket will get full, even if the water flow was different each day.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and reduce risk by avoiding attempts to time the market.
