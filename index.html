📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once, when the market might be high. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term ups and downs. It's a long-term strategy that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like buying groceries every week, regardless of whether your favorite food is on sale or not. You still need to eat, so you buy what you need at the current price, and over time, you average out the costs of the highs and lows.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid emotional decisions, which can lead to a more stable and successful long-term investment strategy.
