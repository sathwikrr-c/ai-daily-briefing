📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once when the market might be high. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when the price is low and fewer shares when the price is high, which can help you save money in the long run. It's a strategy that helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you keep buying the same amount of groceries every week, so when the prices are low, you get more for your money and when they're high, you get less, but overall you're saving money in the long run.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and reduce risk by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
