📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for stocks. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable and stressful.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the prices are high, but sometimes they're low. You still need to eat, so you keep buying the same amount of food every week, regardless of the price. Over time, you'll average out the cost of your groceries, and it's similar with dollar cost averaging in the stock market.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps reduce risk by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
