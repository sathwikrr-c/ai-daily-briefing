📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. This approach can help you build wealth over the long term.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - you turn on the faucet at the same time every day and let it fill up the bucket, regardless of the flow rate, to ensure you have a steady supply of water.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you ride out market ups and downs and build wealth over time.
