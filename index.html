📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once, when the market might be high. By investing a little at a time, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce the impact of market ups and downs. This approach can help you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at regular intervals, regardless of the price of gas. You put in $20 every week, whether gas is expensive or cheap, to ensure you always have enough to get around. Similarly, dollar cost averaging helps you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, smoothing out the ups and downs of the market.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce risk by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
