📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult even for experts.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, but instead of checking prices before you go, you just buy the same amount of food every time. Sometimes you might get a good deal, and sometimes you might pay a bit more, but over time, you'll average out the costs and get a fair price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to more consistent and less stressful investing.
