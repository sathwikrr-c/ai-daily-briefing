📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter how the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable and stressful.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your gas tank at the same time every week, regardless of the price of gas. Sometimes you'll get a good deal, and sometimes you won't, but over time, you'll average out the cost and make sure you have enough gas to get where you need to go.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, reducing your risk and increasing your chances of long-term success.
