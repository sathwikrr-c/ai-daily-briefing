📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for stocks. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on its ups and downs.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the prices are high, sometimes they're low. You still buy the same amount of food every week, because you need it, and over time, the average price you pay will be fair. It's the same with dollar cost averaging - you're buying a fixed amount of stocks regularly, so you'll get some at high prices and some at low prices, but your overall cost will be average.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid emotional decisions, by putting a fixed amount of money into the market at regular intervals, regardless of its performance.
