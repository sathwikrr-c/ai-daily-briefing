📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries every week - you're not worried about the price of milk going up or down, you just buy what you need at the current price and move on. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you're investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce the risk of investing in the stock market and make it easier to stick to your long-term plan.
