📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Market Cap

Market capitalization, or market cap, is the total value of a company's outstanding shares. It's calculated by multiplying the number of shares by the current price of one share. Think of it like the total value of a company if you were to buy all of its shares at the current price. This helps investors understand the size of a company and compare it to others.

Think of it like: Imagine a small lemonade stand versus a huge restaurant chain - the restaurant chain would have a much larger market cap because it's bigger and more valuable, just like how the total value of all the lemonade stands would be much smaller than the total value of all the restaurants.
💬 Remember: Market cap is a simple way to measure a company's size and value, helping you compare and make informed investment decisions.
