📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money by putting a fixed amount of money into an investment at regular intervals. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't try to buy all your food for the year at once, you buy a little each week. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you invest a little money at a time, regardless of the market's performance, just like you buy your groceries regularly, regardless of the price of food.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
