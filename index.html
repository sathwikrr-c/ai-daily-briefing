📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large amount of money at the wrong time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to guess when the market will go up or down. It's a simple and steady approach to investing.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up your gas tank - you put in $20 every week, whether gas prices are high or low, so you don't have to worry about buying gas at the 'right' time.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, reducing your risk and stress.
