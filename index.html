📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for the stock market. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for the price of milk to go down before you buy it, you just buy it every week at the current price, so you have a steady supply. Similarly, dollar cost averaging helps you build a steady portfolio over time, regardless of the market's short-term fluctuations.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps reduce risk by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
