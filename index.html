📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a savings plan, but instead of putting money in a bank, you're putting it into stocks or other investments. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and just focus on investing regularly.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the price of eggs went up one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you'll get a good average price for your groceries.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you smooth out market ups and downs and build wealth over time.
