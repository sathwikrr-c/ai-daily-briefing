📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for investments. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and just focus on slowly building up your investments over time.

Think of it like: It's like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - you just keep the faucet on and let the bucket fill up over time, regardless of the flow rate
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing less stressful.
