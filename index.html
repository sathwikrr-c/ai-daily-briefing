📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when prices are low and fewer shares when prices are high, which can help you save money in the long run. It's a great way for beginners to start investing without worrying about the ups and downs of the market.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for your favorite food to go on sale to buy it, you buy it every week at the same time, regardless of the price. Sometimes you might get a better deal, and sometimes you might pay a bit more, but overall, you'll get a fair price if you keep buying at the same time every week.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest regularly and reduce risk, regardless of the market's performance.
