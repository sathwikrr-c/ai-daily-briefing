📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into an investment at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be tricky. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's a simple and steady approach to growing your money over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water: you add a little bit of water at a time, and eventually, the bucket gets full, regardless of whether it's raining or sunny outside. The steady stream of water represents your regular investments, and the bucket represents your growing wealth.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you smooth out market fluctuations and build wealth over time.
