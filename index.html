📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once when the market might be high. By investing a little bit at a time, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce the impact of ups and downs. It's a long-term strategy that helps you smooth out the bumps in the market.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a hose that sometimes sprays high and sometimes sprays low - if you fill the bucket a little bit at a time, you'll get a steady average level of water in the bucket, rather than trying to fill it all at once when the hose is spraying really high or really low.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can reduce risk and increase long-term returns.
