📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable. By investing a little bit of money at a time, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. This way, you can invest with confidence and not worry about the ups and downs of the market.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up your gas tank - you put in a fixed amount of gas every week, regardless of whether gas prices are high or low, so that you always have enough gas to get around. Over time, the average cost of the gas works out, and you're not affected by the daily ups and downs of gas prices.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest regularly and reduce risk, by averaging out the highs and lows of the market over time.
