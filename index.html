📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money at the wrong time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations. It's a long-term strategy that can help you build wealth over time.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for your favorite food to go on sale before you buy it, you just buy what you need every week. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations, reducing your overall risk.
