📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into the stock market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market, because you're buying more shares when prices are low and fewer shares when prices are high. Over time, this can help reduce the impact of market volatility on your investments. It's a long-term strategy that can help you invest with more confidence.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't try to time the market to buy milk when it's cheapest, you just buy a gallon every week, no matter what. Sometimes you might get a good deal, and sometimes you might pay a bit more, but over time you'll average out to a fair price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest regularly and consistently, reducing the impact of market ups and downs on your investments.
