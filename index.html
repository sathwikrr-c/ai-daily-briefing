📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter how the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very tricky.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries every week - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, and you don't buy extra just because they're low. You just keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you get a good average price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to more stable and consistent returns over the long term.
