📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market and make investing less stressful.

Think of it like: Think of it like regularly adding water to a bucket, regardless of the weather outside - sometimes it's sunny and the water level rises quickly, but on rainy days, the water level might rise more slowly, yet the bucket still fills up over time.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you ride out market ups and downs and build wealth consistently over time.
