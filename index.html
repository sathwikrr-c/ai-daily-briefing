📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market perfectly. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on market ups and downs.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for the prices of your favorite foods to go down before you buy them, you just buy what you need at regular intervals, even if the prices fluctuate.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
