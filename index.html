📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you smooth out the ups and downs of the market and avoid trying to time your investments perfectly. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you build wealth over time. By doing this, you can reduce the impact of market volatility on your investments.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow. You turn on the faucet at the same time every day and let it fill the bucket, regardless of the flow rate. Over time, your bucket will be full, and you won't have worried about the water flow rate each day.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you ride out market ups and downs and build wealth over the long term.
