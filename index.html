📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is an investment strategy where you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter the market's performance. This helps reduce the impact of market volatility on your investments. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when prices are low and fewer shares when prices are high. Over time, this can help you save money and make your investments more stable.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices went up, you continue to buy the same amount every week, and when the prices go down, you get more value for your money
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you ride out market ups and downs and build wealth over time.
