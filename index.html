📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be unpredictable. By investing a little bit of money regularly, you can reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's a simple and steady approach to investing.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like buying groceries every week. You don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, and you don't buy extra just because they're low. You just keep buying what you need, at the current price, and that way you smooth out the ups and downs of food prices over time.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help you ride out market fluctuations and avoid making emotional investment decisions.
