📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. For example, you might invest $100 every month, whether the market is high or low. This way, you'll buy more shares when the market is low and fewer shares when it's high, which can help you save money in the long run.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying what you need and take advantage of sales when they happen. Similarly, dollar cost averaging helps you take advantage of 'sales' in the stock market by consistently investing a fixed amount of money.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you reduce risk by investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
