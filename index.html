📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter how the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to growing your money over time. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market and make investing less stressful.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a faucet that sometimes flows fast and sometimes flows slow - you keep putting in the same amount of water at the same time every day, so even if the faucet flows slow one day, you'll still have a full bucket over time.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance, can help reduce risk and make investing more manageable.
