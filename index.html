📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, consistent savings plan, but for investing in the stock market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the price of your favorite snack went up, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you get a good average price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
