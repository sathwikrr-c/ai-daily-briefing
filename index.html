📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is an investment strategy where you invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter the market's performance. This helps reduce the impact of market volatility on your investments. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when prices are low and fewer shares when prices are high. Over time, this can help you save money and earn more from your investments.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every month, and sometimes the prices are high, while other times they're low. If you keep buying the same amount of groceries every month, you'll get more when prices are low and less when prices are high, which helps you save money in the long run.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and reduce the risk of investing in the stock market by averaging out the highs and lows over time.
