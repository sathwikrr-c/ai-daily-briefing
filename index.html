📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't try to buy all your food for the year in one trip, you go to the store every week and buy what you need, regardless of whether prices are high or low. This way, you smooth out the costs and avoid having to guess when prices will be low.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid timing risks, making it a great strategy for long-term investors.
