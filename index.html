📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once when the market might be high. Over time, this approach can help you buy more shares when prices are low and fewer shares when prices are high. It's a disciplined way to invest that can help you ride out market ups and downs.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and sometimes you get a better deal, sometimes you don't, but overall you get what you need
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and reduce risk by removing the need to perfectly time the market.
