📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market, which can be very difficult even for experts.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the prices are high, sometimes they're low. But you still need to buy your groceries, so you just keep buying the same amount every week, regardless of the price. Over time, you'll average out the costs and get a good deal overall.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to more stable and long-term financial growth.
