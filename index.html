📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into a stock or fund at regular intervals, no matter what the price is. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and make emotional decisions based on short-term price changes. It's a steady and consistent approach to investing.

Think of it like: Think of dollar cost averaging like filling up your car's gas tank at the same time every week, regardless of the price of gas. Sometimes you'll get a good deal, and sometimes you won't, but over time, you'll average out the cost and make sure you always have enough gas to get where you need to go.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
