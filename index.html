📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once when the market might be high. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce the impact of market ups and downs. It's a simple and steady approach to investing.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't wait for the price of milk to go down before you buy it, you just buy it every week at the current price. Over time, you'll average out the cost of milk, and it's the same with investing.
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing more manageable.
