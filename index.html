📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting a fixed amount of money into the market at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the price of your favorite snack goes up or down, you just keep buying what you need at a steady pace. Similarly, with dollar cost averaging, you keep investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market changes.
