📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing money in the stock market where you put in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing a large sum of money all at once when the market might be high. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and reduce stress. It's a simple and steady approach to investing.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace, and over time, you get a fair average price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to a more stable and long-term investing approach.
