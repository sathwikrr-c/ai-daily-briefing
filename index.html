📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest money in the stock market by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you avoid trying to time the market. By doing this, you can invest without worrying about whether the market is high or low.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, and sometimes the prices are high, sometimes they're low. You still buy the same amount of food every week, regardless of the price, because you need it. Dollar cost averaging works the same way, helping you build up your investments over time by consistently buying a fixed amount, no matter what.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, reducing the risk of making emotional investment decisions.
