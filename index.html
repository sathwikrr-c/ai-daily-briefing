📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way of investing where you put a fixed amount of money into a stock or fund at regular intervals, no matter what the price is. This helps reduce the impact of market ups and downs on your investments. It's like a steady, long-term plan that helps you smooth out the bumps in the market. By doing this, you can avoid trying to time the market and just focus on investing regularly.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the price of milk went up one week, you keep buying what you need at a steady pace. Similarly, dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily, regardless of the market's ups and downs.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid trying to time the market, reducing the risk of making emotional investment decisions.
