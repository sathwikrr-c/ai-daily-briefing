📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on market ups and downs.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you don't stop buying because the prices are high one week, you keep buying the same amount because you need them. It's the same with dollar cost averaging, you keep investing the same amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market fluctuations.
