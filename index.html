📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to building wealth over time. By doing this, you can smooth out the ups and downs of the market and make investing less stressful.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water: you add a little water at a time, and eventually, the bucket gets full, regardless of how much it rained or shined on any given day.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and reduce risk by removing the need to perfectly time the market.
