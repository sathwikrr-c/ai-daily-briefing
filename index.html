📚 Learn: Stock Market
📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter what the market is doing. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at the wrong time. It's like a steady, consistent approach to investing, rather than trying to time the market. By doing this, you can avoid making emotional decisions based on short-term market swings.

Think of it like: Imagine you're buying groceries every week, you put $100 in your grocery fund each month, whether apples are on sale or not. You're not trying to buy all your groceries when they're cheapest, you're just consistently putting money in, so over time, you get a good average price.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest consistently and avoid trying to time the market, which can lead to lower risk and more stable long-term returns.
