📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter the market's performance. This helps reduce the risk of investing all your money at once when the market is high. By doing this, you'll buy more shares when the price is low and fewer shares when the price is high, which can help you save money in the long run. It's a strategy that helps you invest steadily and avoid making emotional decisions based on market ups and downs.

Think of it like: Think of it like buying groceries - you don't stop buying food just because the prices are high one week, you keep buying the same amount every week, and when the prices are low, you get more value for your money.
💬 Remember: Dollar cost averaging helps you invest steadily and avoid timing risks by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, regardless of the market's performance.
