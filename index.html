📖 Today's Concept: Dollar Cost Averaging

Dollar cost averaging is a way to invest your money by putting in a fixed amount of money at regular intervals, no matter how the market is doing. This helps you avoid trying to time the market, which can be really hard. By investing a little bit at a time, you can reduce the risk of putting all your money in at the wrong time. It's a way to make investing more stable and less stressful.

Think of it like: Think of it like filling up a bucket with water from a hose that sometimes sprays a lot and sometimes just drips - you're still putting in the same amount of water each week, even if the hose is spraying more or less, and over time, your bucket will get full
💬 Remember: Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals can help reduce risk and make investing more stable.
